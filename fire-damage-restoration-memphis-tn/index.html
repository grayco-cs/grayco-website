Fire damage? What to do in the first 24 hours

  1. Wait for the all-clearDo not go back inside until the fire department says the building is safe. Fire weakens floors, roofs and stairs, and hidden hot spots can flare up.
  2. Call GrayCo 24/7: (901) 585-0944We board up broken windows and doors and tarp holes in the roof so weather, animals and intruders stay out. Securing the property is normally expected by your policy.
  3. Photograph before anything is movedTake photos and video of every room, including smoke stains on walls and ceilings and damaged belongings. Do not throw anything away until it is documented.
  4. Do not wipe soot or run the HVACSoot smears and can set into paint, fabric and grout when you wipe it. Keep the heating and air off until the system is checked — it can spread soot and odor through the whole house.
  5. Deal with the water tooFirefighting leaves a lot of water in walls, floors and ceilings. It needs the same extraction and drying as a burst pipe, starting in the first day or two.
  6. Report the claimCall your insurance company, write down the claim number, and ask about additional living expenses if you cannot stay in the home. Keep every receipt.
  7. Get a written scopeWe inspect the structure, contents areas and smoke spread, and give you a written scope for cleanup and rebuild that you can share with your adjuster.

Fire damage restoration in Memphis: frequently asked questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency board-up after a fire in Memphis?

Yes. Call (901) 585-0944 day or night once the fire department has cleared the scene. We board up openings and tarp the roof to protect the house until repairs start.

Smoke spread through my whole older Memphis house. Can it be cleaned?

Often, yes. Plaster, trim and framing can usually be cleaned and sealed; porous materials that hold odor may need to be replaced. We test and explain each area in the written scope.

Is fire damage covered by homeowners insurance?

Fire is a covered cause of loss in many homeowners policies, but what is paid depends on your own policy, limits and deductible. We document the fire, smoke and water damage thoroughly so your claim reflects everything that was damaged; we never promise coverage we cannot control.

Can smoke odor really be removed?

In most homes, yes. Odor is removed by cleaning or removing the materials that hold it, cleaning the HVAC system, and sealing affected framing before rebuilding — not by masking it with fragrance.

Yes. GrayCo is a licensed general contractor with an unlimited license (TN #85034), so the same company handles the board-up, cleanup and drying, then the reconstruction — framing, roof, drywall, electrical and plumbing coordination, cabinets, floors and paint. No hand-off.
