Storm damage? What to do in the first 24 hours

  1. Get safeStay away from downed power lines and report them to the utility. Keep everyone out of rooms with ceiling damage or a tree through the roof.
  2. Call GrayCo 24/7: (901) 585-0944We tarp the roof or board up broken windows and doors to keep rain out. Emergency protection is normally expected by your policy to prevent further damage.
  3. Photograph the damageFrom the ground (never climb on a wet roof), photograph the roof, gutters, siding, windows, fences, cars and any hail stones next to a coin or ruler for size. Inside, photograph ceiling stains and wet areas.
  4. Catch the water insidePut buckets under drips, move furniture and electronics, and poke a small hole in a ceiling bulge only if it is safe — water trapped above drywall can bring the ceiling down.
  5. Report the claimCall your insurance company, write down the claim number and the adjuster contact, and keep receipts for anything you spend (tarps, hotel, food).
  6. Watch out for storm chasersDo not sign anything at the door after a storm. Check for a Tennessee contractor license and a local address — GrayCo is licensed (TN #85034) and based in Bartlett.
  7. Get a free inspectionWe inspect the roof and exterior, document every damaged area, and give you a written report before the adjuster visits.
